### New
- **Inputs on Home.** An HDMI port as a tile on Home, named after its device. Select **Talkies settings → Inputs on Home**.
- **Open an app at power-on.** HDMI 1 or any app opens each time the TV turns on. Select **Talkies settings → At power-on**.
- **Lite effects.** No glow and no gliding, for slow TVs. Auto picks it on TVs with under 1.6 GB of memory. Select **Talkies settings → Effects**.
- **Report a problem.** A QR code opens a bug report on your phone, details filled in. Select **Talkies settings → About**.
- **What's new.** This list, once after each update. Select **Talkies settings → About → What's new** to see it again.
- **Weather icon.** A small drawing of the sky beside the temperature.

### Changed
- **Cleaner focus.** The card you're on grows and lifts on a soft shadow, with no white ring around it.
- **Cricket without a key.** Scores now come from ESPN, like football, and refresh every minute during a match.

### Fixed
- **Finish setup on older TVs.** It no longer gets stuck on Notification access when the TV has no such screen.
- **No blank picks.** Picks no longer go blank for a moment as you move along them.
- **First cards in full.** The first card in a row is no longer cut off at the left when you're on it.

### Removed
- **Free up memory.** Android lets an app close only its own background apps, so it couldn't free anything.
