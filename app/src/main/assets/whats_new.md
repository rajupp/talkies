### New
- **Picks without a key in India.** Each app's Top 10 and what's just come out, from StreamRadar. Select **Talkies settings → Picks from** to switch between TMDB and StreamRadar.
- **NBA and NFL scores.** Live games, start times and results, below the dock. Select **Talkies settings → Sports**.
- **Sports.** Turn Cricket, Football, NBA and NFL on or off one by one. Select **Talkies settings → Sports**.
- **Country.** Picks and scores for any country, not just where the TV is. Select **Talkies settings → Country**.

### Changed
- **Settings in groups.** Talkies settings has five sections now: Look, For You and scores, Apps and inputs, Remote and power, and Talkies.

### Fixed
- **Picks on patchy networks.** A dropped connection to TMDB is tried again, so the picks don't wait half an hour.
