### New
- **A fresh look.** The focused card has a light outline, match cards are glass like the dock, and menus are cleaner. Pick Outline, Lift or Ring in **Talkies settings → Focus**.
- **Football during international breaks.** The Nations League and international friendlies are now in Football leagues.
- **Tidy Recent apps.** Press Down on a card for **Remove from list** and **App info**.
- **Remote keys knows more buttons** on Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips, Xiaomi and Google TV Streamer remotes.

### Fixed
- **New and removed apps show up on Home right away.**
- **Continue watching gets its pictures** after the TV restarts.
- **At power-on, the app or input you chose stays open.**
- **A game controller's buttons are the game's**, not remote shortcuts.
